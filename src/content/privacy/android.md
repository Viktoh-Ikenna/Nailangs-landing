# Privacy Policy — Nailangs Keyboard

**Last updated: 26 August 2026**

This policy explains how the Nailangs Keyboard app for Android (`ng.nailangs.keyboard01`)
handles your information.

## The short version

Nailangs Keyboard does not collect your data. What you type stays on your phone. There is
no Nailangs account, no server that receives your keystrokes, and no analytics or
advertising software in the app.

## What happens to what you type

Nailangs Keyboard is an input method — by design, it can see the text you type while it is
the active keyboard. That text is processed entirely on your device, in memory, in order to
show word suggestions.

We do not transmit, upload, store on a server, sell, or share the text you type. It never
leaves your phone.

## Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its own private storage, readable only by the app:

- **A dictionary of words**, shipped with the app and used to generate suggestions.
- **Words you reinforce.** When you pick a word from the suggestion bar or complete a word
  with the space bar, the app increases that word's ranking so it appears sooner next time.
  This is stored in a local database file on your device.
- **Your settings** — the selected theme, and whether key sounds and haptic feedback are on.
- **Recently used emoji**, so they appear first in the emoji list.

All of this is removed when you uninstall the app. None of it is sent anywhere.

## Information we collect

None. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising software, and no
in-app purchases. We do not create a profile of you and we have no way to identify you.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet / network state** — used only to open web links (this policy, and the Nailangs
  website) in your browser, and for the Google Play in-app review prompt. It is not used to
  transmit anything you type.
- **Read and write user dictionary** — lets the keyboard read and add to the personal
  dictionary that Android maintains, so words you have taught your phone are recognised.
  This dictionary belongs to your device and is not read by us.

## Third-party services

The app uses Google Play's in-app review feature, which may show a rating prompt. It is
provided by Google and governed by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). It does not receive anything
you type.

The app contains no advertising software and no payment processing.

## Children

Nailangs Keyboard is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no data at all,
we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
