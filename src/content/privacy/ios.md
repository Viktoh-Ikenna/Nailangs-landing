# Privacy Policy — Nailangs Keyboard for iPhone and iPad

**Draft for review — last updated: 12 November 2026**

## The short version

Nailangs Keyboard does not collect your data. What you type stays on your iPhone or iPad. The
keyboard does not ask for Full Access, so iOS does not let it connect to the internet at all.
There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

## What happens to what you type

While Nailangs is the active keyboard it sees the text you type, which it needs in order to
show word suggestions. That text is processed on your device only. It is never transmitted,
uploaded, sold or shared.

## Information stored on your device

- **A dictionary of words**, shipped with the app.
- **Words you use**, and which words you use together, so suggestions follow how you write.
  Nothing is learned in password, email or web address fields.
- **Your settings** — theme, key sounds, haptics and language.
- **Recently used emoji.**

All of this stays in the app's private storage on your device and is removed when you delete
the app.

## Information we collect

None. The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or in-app purchases.

## Children

Because we collect no data at all, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone,
including children.
