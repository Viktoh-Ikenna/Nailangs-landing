# Privacy Policy — Nailangs for Mac

**Draft for review — last updated: 12 November 2026**

## The short version

Nailangs for Mac is a keyboard layout and a companion app that installs it. It does not
collect, store or share any personal data, and it has no accounts, analytics or advertising.

## What you type

The Nailangs layout is a standard macOS keyboard layout: macOS turns your key presses into
Nailangs letters, the same way it does for any other layout. Neither the layout nor the
companion app records or sends what you type.

## Data we collect

None.
